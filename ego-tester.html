What this tests

Provocation is the mirror.

The point is not to prove that someone has a “big ego.” The point is to observe what changes when self-image is threatened: reasoning, accountability, blame, status defense, escalation, or withdrawal.

Some scenarios are deliberately unfair, confrontational, or irritating. That is intentional. A neutral question can reveal what you believe. A provocative question can reveal how you defend it.

Important: This is a self-reflection game, not a clinical or psychological diagnosis. A high score in any dimension means the simulated responses leaned toward that pattern in this test.
